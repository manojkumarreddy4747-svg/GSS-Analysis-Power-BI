# Global Superstore – Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze global sales, profitability, product performance, and business growth trends.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify high-performing regions and product categories.
- Compare sales growth across MoM, QoQ, and YoY.
- Analyze customer segments and product contribution.
- Identify loss-making products and profitability trends.

## Key Performance Indicators
| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 12.64M |
| Total Profit | 1.47M |
| Total Orders | 25K |
| Total Customers | 2K |
| Profit Margin | 11.61% |
| Total Quantity | 178K |
